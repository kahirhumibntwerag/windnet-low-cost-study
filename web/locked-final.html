MSE skill = 1 − model MSE / reference MSE; positive is better. Δ RMSE = model − reference; negative is better. “Common finite n” is before the observed-speed threshold; “Scored n” is after it.     06 · Interpretation & provenance

    What this panel can establish

    Freeze before opening

    The model recipes, 15-member panel, primary roles, thresholds, split rules, and reporting code are frozen before final target opening. No winner, adoption decision, or new recipe is selected from these results.

    The original five fold assignments and offsets 6–19 define evaluation windows. Fitting uses offsets 6–18 in other folds, with source-fold masking and duplicate-target block purging. Released WindNet forecasts are fixed references.

    Historical evidence, with explicit limits

    Folds 1–4 informed model development, and original Fold 5 was already seen in the earlier campaign. All-fold averages are exploratory historical comparisons; they do not turn this study into a newly untouched confirmation.

    P0 is faithful retraining of the earlier champion recipe under stricter isolation. The actual previous frozen campaign winner is a separate reference. Present-day calibrated SDO/EVE features do not prove real-time operational availability.
