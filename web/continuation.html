03 · What was measured

Data provenance & evaluation protocol

A

Derived SDO/EVE features

Public LASP Level 3 tables provide scalar irradiances from 6 emission-line channels and 3 diode channels. No SDO images are downloaded or used by this continuation.

The 62 derived inputs contain log irradiance at target-day lags 5–8, four-day means, endpoint trends, and two channel log-ratios. A two-day publication guard is applied relative to the nominal target-minus-three-day issue.

These are present-day calibrated science products, not an archive of what was available in real time. Instrument gaps remain missing. Full-disk irradiance does not preserve spatial coronal-hole geometry.

Preflight revision: H7 was cancelled before fitting. H8 omits diode1216 because its Fourier-filter timing is not established, and diode366 because the product is marked unstable.

B

Original holdout kept outside development

Only original Folds 1–4 enter the continuation bundle. Original Fold 5 targets and predictions are absent, and predictor measurements sourced from Fold 5 are masked.

Inner CV rotates the validation fold through 1–4. Whole fitting blocks that duplicate a validation target date are purged, and measurements sourced from the inner validation fold are masked before fitting.

Fitting keeps original offsets 6–18; validation retains all fourteen offsets 6–19. Preprocessing is fitted within each training split. Results pool the original validation windows.

This page reports model selection, not a fresh WindNet test comparison. A valid independent performance claim requires a future untouched evaluation.

A fixed decision rule

An EVE candidate must keep overall RMSE within 1% of the wind-only incumbent and reduce observed ≥500 km/s RMSE by at least 2%. Eligible candidates minimize an equally weighted normalized overall/fast-wind MSE objective. If none qualify, the incumbent is retained. The ≥600 km/s threshold is secondary and does not select the model.

Comparison with WindNet

The original fold assignments and evaluation windows anchor the study. These continuation CV scores cannot be compared numerically with WindNet’s published holdout scores as if they used the same cases. The wind-only incumbent and causal persistence forecasts provide the matched development comparisons here.
